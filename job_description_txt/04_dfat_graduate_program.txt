Title: DFAT 2027 Graduate Program (Corporate and Policy streams) — Registrations for 2028 program open (2027 program closed)
Employer: Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade (DFAT)
Source: https://www.dfat.gov.au/careers/dfat-aps-careers/graduate-program
Location: Canberra, ACT (relocation assistance available)
Office arrangement: On-site
Duration: 18 months
Salary: APS4 $84,115 (commencement) rising to APS5 $92,753 (completion), + 15.4% super
Commencement date: February 2027
Employment type: Ongoing APS, full-time
Security clearance: Negative Vetting Level 2

Description:
DFAT manages Australia's international presence — a network of over 116 embassies, high commissions, consulates-general and representative offices across five continents, with over 6,000 staff in Australia and overseas. The 18-month graduate program has two streams: Corporate and Policy. Graduates complete 4 placements across a range of government priorities.

Corporate graduates may be placed in roles such as: consular and crisis management, overseas property office, accounting and financial management, HR, diplomatic security, strategic communications, ICT, corporate law, Australian Passport Office, international operations.

Policy graduates may be placed in roles such as: bilateral economic and political relations, international trade negotiations and economic analysis, development and aid program management, international security and cyber security, international law, public diplomacy, multilateral and global issues, strategic communications.

Eligibility criteria:
- Must meet DFAT general eligibility requirements to apply.
- Be an Australian Citizen at the time of application.
- Have completed at least a bachelor's degree between 1 January 2022 and 31 December 2026.
- Prior to starting the program, successful candidates need to obtain an Australian national security clearance at Negative Vetting Level 2.
- Ongoing employment requires obtaining and maintaining a Negative Vetting 2 security clearance; loss of clearance at any time leads to termination of employment.
- Can apply from overseas, but the security interview must be conducted in Australia (return at own expense if overseas during vetting).
- If only qualification is from an overseas institution, must provide Australian documentation recognising the qualification(s).

Skills needed:
Genuine interest in international affairs and trade. Ability to work collaboratively, manage relationships, humility, drive, accountability. Adaptability, resilience, analytical thinking, problem-solving.

Application process: Not detailed on this page — see "Current Opportunities" page for how to apply. Queries directed to DFP Recruitment (dfat.graduates@dfp.com.au / 1300 184 817).
