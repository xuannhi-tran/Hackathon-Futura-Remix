Description:
Innisfail Medical Centre is a privately-billing, established General Practice in Innisfail, QLD. Staffed by 5 fellowed GPs, 2 trainee GP registrars, 1 full-time RN/soon-to-be Nurse Practitioner, 2 part-time RNs. Pride ourselves on fostering positive environment supporting learning and growth. Aiming to grow workforce to meet demand for quality General Practice in community.

Nurses work collaboratively with GPs to look after broad spectrum of patients — "Cradle to Grave" medicine. Training and support provided; prior General Practice experience not required — happy to help you upskill into rewarding role.

Key responsibilities:
- Administering injections and immunisations, including children (upskilling course supported)
- Assisting with procedures — skin cancer excisions, Mirena/Implanon insertions
- Running iron and Aclasta infusions
- Wound care, both simple and complex
- Preventative health promotion and chronic disease management — preparation of health assessments, GP Chronic Disease Management Plans
- Holter monitors, ECGs, audiology
- Recalling patients for results, responding to phone calls from patients
