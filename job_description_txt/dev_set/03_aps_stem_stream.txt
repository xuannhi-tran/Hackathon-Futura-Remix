Title: STEM Stream — Australian Government Graduate Program (AGGP)
Employer: Department of Industry, Science, and Resources (DISR), on behalf of Australian Government / Commonwealth agencies
Source: https://content.apsjobs.gov.au/career-pathways/graduate-programs/stem-stream
Location: Multiple locations (most in Canberra; limited spots across Australia)
Office arrangement: On-site, remote or hybrid
Duration: Full time, 12 months
Salary: $78,770 - $85,027
Applications open: 1 March 2027

Description:
As a STEM graduate, you'll gain hands-on experience working on real-world projects that contribute to Australia's future. Managed by the Department of Industry, Science, and Resources (DISR), the STEM Stream recruits graduates across a range of Australian Government and Commonwealth agencies.

Eligibility criteria:
To be eligible for the STEM Stream in the Australian Government Graduate Program (AGGP), you must submit your application with evidence of the following:
- Have completed an Australian Qualifications Framework Level 7 qualification (a Bachelor degree), or higher equivalent by 31 December 2026. Relevant degrees are from the science, technology, engineering, or mathematics fields.
- Have completed your most recent eligible qualification between 1 January 2022 to 31 December 2026.
- Be an Australian citizen at the time of application.
- Be able to obtain and maintain a valid Australian Government security clearance, when required. For more information, refer to the Australian Government Security Vetting Agency (AGSVA).
- Be willing to undergo any police, character, health, or other checks required.

Skills needed:
Proficient communication and problem-solving skills. Willingness to learn and grow. Ability to work collaboratively in a team environment.

Application process: Initial application (resume, academic transcript, proof of Australian citizenship, agency/location preferences) → assessment (scenario-based assessments, panel interviews) → reference checks → matching and offers (merit pool).
