Description:
Velocity Legal is looking for a Law Graduate to join the Employment Team. You will work directly with the team on real matters involving employment advice, workplace disputes, employment contracts, workplace investigations, termination of employment.

Help clients navigate workplace challenges and understand their options. Develop skills across advisory work and disputes while learning to balance legal obligations with commercial priorities and the human side of workplace issues. Learn how to think carefully, write clearly, give practical advice. For the right graduate, opportunity to build specialist expertise early.

Work full-time from Melbourne CBD office.

Your work will include:
- Conducting legal research on employment and workplace relations issues
- Preparing draft advice, correspondence, file notes
- Assisting with employment contracts, workplace policies, settlement documents
- Researching modern award coverage and employment entitlements
- Assisting with advice on performance management, disciplinary processes, termination
- Supporting workplace investigations and addressing sensitive employee complaints
- Assisting with unfair dismissal, general protections, underpayment disputes
- Preparing chronologies, evidence, tribunal and court documents, briefs to counsel
- Attending client conferences, conciliations, mediations, hearings where appropriate
- Assisting with fee estimates, client onboarding, file management
- Supporting team with day-to-day conduct of client matters

You will receive direct feedback, proper supervision, exposure to high-quality work from start.
