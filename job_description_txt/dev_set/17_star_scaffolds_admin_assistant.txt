Description:
Looking for your first step into a rewarding career in administration?

Join a friendly, growing team where you'll be supported, trained and given the opportunity to develop your skills.

Based in our Sunshine Coast branch at Bells Creek, you'll gain experience across a range of administration and scheduling activities:
- Answering incoming calls/emails
- General office work including data entry, photocopying, laminating, etc.
- Assisting other departments with day-to-day operations
- Scheduling and confirming bookings for our customers.
- Liaising with installers and sub-contractors
- Reviewing install/dismantle photos and scheduling rectifications as required.
- Uploading of purchase orders and other documents
- Any task which may be required, in relation to administration duties required for the business.

A proven commitment to safety, quality and service excellence has seen us become the preferred supplier to some of the most respected construction and engineering firms in Queensland and Victoria. We are a stable, professional and respected company.
