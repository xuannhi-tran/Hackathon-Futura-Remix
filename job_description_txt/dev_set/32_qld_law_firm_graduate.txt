Description:
A small, growing Queensland law practice is looking for a final-year law student, law graduate or newly admitted lawyer to join the team from January 2027.

"We are not a traditional law firm, and this is not a traditional graduate role." Not a high-end corporate environment with suits and impressive legal language clients don't understand. Friendly, approachable, down-to-earth. Clients choose us because we provide excellent legal services while remaining relatable, accessible, easy to talk to. Explain complex issues in plain English, treat clients as people, place exceptional service at forefront.

Genuinely hands-on early-career role. Join close, supportive team; work directly with experienced owner/solicitors. Receive high-quality practical training from beginning. Actively involved, challenged to think, supported as you develop judgment, confidence, professional skills.

Role initially focuses on residential conveyancing and property law. Trained to understand and take responsibility for matters from beginning to end, not isolated tasks. As you become proficient, experience expands into other areas: estate planning, broader property law, family law, commercial matters.

Opportunity to use full range of skills from law degree and build practical, commercial, interpersonal skills university cannot teach.

Who may apply?
Candidates who:
- Are in final 12 months of Australian law degree and able to work full-time
- Have completed law degree and preparing to commence Practical Legal Training
- Are currently completing Practical Legal Training
- Have recently completed Practical Legal Training
- Have been admitted as Australian lawyer within approximately previous six months

Previous conveyancing experience helpful but not essential. Attitude, judgment, attention to detail, willingness to learn matter more.
