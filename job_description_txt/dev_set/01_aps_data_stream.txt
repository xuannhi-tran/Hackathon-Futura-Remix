Title: Data Stream — Australian Government Graduate Program (AGGP)
Employer: Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS), on behalf of Australian Government / Commonwealth agencies
Source: https://content.apsjobs.gov.au/career-pathways/graduate-programs/data-stream
Location: Multiple locations (Canberra, Adelaide, Brisbane, Melbourne, Perth, Sydney; limited spots also Albury, Alice Springs, Darwin, Geelong, Hobart, Newcastle, Richmond, Robina, Townsville)
Office arrangement: Hybrid and on-site
Duration: Full time, 12 months
Salary: $69,761 - $94,270
Applications open: 1 March 2027

Description:
As a data graduate, you will contribute to data-driven projects that support policy, improve services and inform decision-making across government. Managed by the Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS), the Data Stream is recruiting more than 170 graduate roles across the Australian Government and Commonwealth agencies.

Eligibility criteria:
To be eligible for the Data Stream in the Australian Government Graduate Program (AGGP), you must submit your application with evidence of the following:
- Have completed at least an Australian Qualifications Framework Level 7 qualification (a Bachelor degree), or higher equivalent by 31 December 2026.
- Have completed your most recent eligible qualification between 1 January 2022 to 31 December 2026.
- Be an Australian citizen at the time of application.
- Be able to obtain and maintain a valid Australian Government security clearance if required.
- Be willing to undergo any police, character, health, or other checks required.

Skills needed:
Strong data technical skills. Strong communication and collaboration skills. Critical thinking and problem-solving skills.

Application process: Initial application → online assessment (Data Fundamentals Task + Situational Judgement Task) → interview → reference checks → merit pool matching → offer.
