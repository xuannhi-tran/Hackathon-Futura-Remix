Description:
Ready to launch your career in the South Australian Public Sector?

The Department of Treasury and Finance (DTF) is seeking passionate and driven Graduate Officers to join our dynamic department in information technology and cyber security fields. If you're ready for a career with purpose and impact, where you can shape the future of South Australia, then joining the public sector could be the pathway for you.

You will ignite, evolve and excel in a public sector career that offers diverse opportunities and clear pathways for advancement. You'll have access to valuable training and development with support from mentors and supervisors, exposure and networking across departments and agencies, and meaningful work.

Successful applicants will be placed in a merit pool to fill vacancies across SA Government sector over a 12-month period.

What we offer:
- Meaningful work that contributes to outcomes for South Australia
- A supportive team environment where people connect and collaborate
- Flexible working arrangements that support life and career
- Opportunities to grow capability and build a long-term career
- Salary packaging and wellbeing benefits

Who we are
At the DTF, we're building a world-class Treasury for South Australia – it's about the people and the impact we create together. We are South Australia's lead agency for economic, digital and financial policy outcomes.

What you will do
Create meaningful impact, support critical government digital infrastructure and processes and immerse yourself in real-world experiences, setting yourself up for a career in one of the following pathways:
- Information and communication technology (ICT)
- Cyber security and resilience

Why join this Graduate Program?
You will join a Graduate Program through the Academy, offering opportunities for career development including: unique and tailored experiences, the opportunity to have impact, career development, collaborative culture, mentorship, and commitment and flexibility.

Eligibility

To be considered eligible to become a Graduate Officer, you must meet the following criteria:

Availability: The roles commence in February 2027.

Location: You will be working within metropolitan Adelaide, with many roles located in the Adelaide CBD.

Qualifications: You must have completed an undergraduate or post graduate Australian University qualification, or you will have completed a qualification before your commencement date in a Graduate Officer role. A relevant Certificate IV or Diploma will also be accepted. Preferred disciplines include information technology, information systems, cyber security, computer science, software engineering, data analytics, artificial intelligence and related disciplines.

History: You must not have received a Targeted Voluntary Separation Package from the SA public sector within the last 12 months.

Background Check: You must be willing to undergo relevant criminal history check(s) and receive clearance to meet the required entry standards.

Visa Holders: You must hold a VISA with acceptable work conditions from the commencement date of your employment.

Employment Conditions: You must meet all other employment conditions, including the ability to travel independently to and from the workplace, training sites, and other sites as required, and some out-of-hours work may be required.

Current Salary
Year 1: ASO3: $74,095 per annum, plus 12% employer super contributions
Year 2: ASO4: $82,212 per annum, plus 12% employer super contributions

Graduate roles start at ASO3. International students will initially be offered a temporary contract with the option to transition to an ongoing role once they have secured permanent residency. All graduates have the opportunity to transition to the ASO4 level after 12 months upon successful completion of the program.

Application Instructions
Join DTF as a Graduate Officer by submitting your application through I WORK FOR SA, including: a covering letter, resume with three referees, academic transcript, a completed 'Pre-Employment Declaration' form, and VISA holders are to provide evidence of your visa, including any working conditions that apply to your visa.

Diversity & Inclusion

**KEY SIGNALS (this is the first ad in the corpus with an explicitly WELCOMING international-student/visa policy, not just silence or a blocker):

1. EXPLICIT PRO-INTERNATIONAL-STUDENT PATHWAY: "International students will initially be offered a temporary contract with the option to transition to an ongoing role once they have secured permanent residency." This is a rare, genuinely favourable disclosure — most ads either say nothing (silent Apply) or state a hard/conditional visa requirement framed as a screening hurdle. Here the employer proactively explains HOW international students fit into the hiring structure (temp contract → ongoing role once PR secured), which is closer to a green flag than any other ad collected so far. Good test case for whether the rules engine can distinguish "we exclude temp visa holders" (HNE Transition pathway, #07) from "we have a defined pathway for temp visa/international student hires" (this ad) — both mention visas but carry opposite practical implications for the student.

2. VISA_WORK_CONDITIONS conditional: "You must hold a VISA with acceptable work conditions from the commencement date of your employment" — not a citizenship/PR requirement, and not a blanket temp-visa exclusion; it's a generic "your visa conditions must permit this work" clause, softer than the ACCIONA/Gold Coast "full working rights" framing and much softer than the EY/Boeing citizenship-mandatory framing.

3. Government (state, SA) sector, ICT/cyber security field — fills a gap (no state government ICT ad yet; prior government entries were APS federal, local council, and this is state-level).

4. "Some out-of-hours work may be required" — a mild hours-flexibility note, distinct from the explicit weekly-hours figures in Gold Coast (#04) and HNE (#07).**
