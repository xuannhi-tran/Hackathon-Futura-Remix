Title: Generalist Stream — Australian Government Graduate Program (AGGP)
Employer: Australian Public Service Commission (APSC), on behalf of Australian Government / Commonwealth agencies
Source: https://content.apsjobs.gov.au/career-pathways/graduate-programs/generalist-stream
Location: Multiple locations (most in Canberra; limited spots nationwide)
Office arrangement: In-person, remote, hybrid
Duration: 12 months, full-time
Salary: $70,000 - $89,000
Applications open: 1 March 2027

Description:
As a generalist graduate, you will work on policy and programs to support the design, development, and delivery of government initiatives. Managed by the Australian Public Service Commission (APSC), the Generalist Stream recruits graduates across a range of Australian Government and Commonwealth agencies.

Eligibility criteria:
To be eligible for the Generalist Stream in the Australian Government Graduate Program (AGGP), you must submit your application with evidence of the following:
- Have completed an Australian Qualifications Framework Level 7 qualification (Bachelor degree), or higher equivalent between 1 January 2022 and 31 December 2026.
- Be an Australian citizen at the time of application.
- Be willing to undergo police, character, health, or other checks as required.
- Be able to obtain and maintain a valid Australian Government security clearance when required. For more information, refer to the Australian Government Vetting Agency (AGSVA).

Skills needed:
Analytical and critical thinking skills. Strong written and verbal communication skills. Interpersonal and collaboration skills. Problem-solving skills and sound judgement. Adaptability and willingness to learn.

Application process: Initial application (~6 weeks) → assessments — online aptitude tests, written pitch, one-way video interview (~2 months) → reference checks (~1 month) → preferences and matching, merit pool (~2 months) → receive offers (~6 weeks).
