2027 KPMG Graduate Program – Business, Consulting & Risk - Rolling Intake

Help organisations tackle some of their biggest challenges. From improving customer experiences and redesigning services, to strengthening governance, evaluating program outcomes, and supporting transformation. You’ll work alongside specialists across consulting, technology, operations and risk to uncover insights, explore solutions and deliver work that creates meaningful impact. This pathway suits graduates who enjoy solving problems, working with people, understanding how things work and contributing to positive change.

About rolling intake: We run two rounds of recruitment: main intake (now closed) and rolling intake. We aim to fill as many of our roles as possible through the main intake but, of course, things change and new roles come up. Applying to the rolling intake means your application will be reviewed as a suitable role comes up (and it does happen!).

The Work

Depending on the team, your work might include:

- Reviewing business processes and identifying opportunities for improvement
- Assessing governance frameworks, risk controls and compliance practices
- Helping design or evaluate government programs and public services
- Mapping customer journeys or analysing operational data
- Supporting organisational change, stakeholder engagement or transformation projects
- Contributing to digital, technology or advisory initiatives that enable better outcomes

You’ll gain exposure to a wide range of industries and project types, helping you build strong consulting skills and a deep understanding of how organisations work.

About the Graduate Program

- For students in their final year of study in 2026 or recently graduated and not yet in professional work.
- A one‑year grow pathway with targeted coaching, support and development as you begin your career.
- Real client work from the start, so you can make an impact and learn straightaway.
- Start work in February 2027 (some teams may start slightly earlier or later) in a full‑time, ongoing graduate role.

How to apply

This ad represents teams in our Business, Consulting, Risk & ESG pathway, but we hire across a wide range of areas. Whether you already know the team you want to apply for or you’re still exploring, you’ll be able to select a pathway (the broad grouping of teams), a segment (such as audit, risk or cyber) KPMG Division, or a specific team.

Not sure or open to something new? Choose no preference and we’ll match you to the teams that best fit your degree and interests.
