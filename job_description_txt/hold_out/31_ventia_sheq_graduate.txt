Real work. Real impact. Our Graduate Program.

Our Ventia Graduate Program is designed to turn potential into performance. This tailored two-year program provides practical experience across diverse, live projects through four structured one on one rotations. You'll gain exposure to strategic SHEQ governance, systems, performance and business-wide initiatives, develop core SHEQ capabilities in lower-risk operational environments, and build critical risk management and safety leadership skills in complex, high-consequence settings.

In just two years, you’ll gain access to tailored professional development, blending technical training and essential soft skills to fast-track your transition from university to industry. Supported by senior mentors and a dedicated graduate buddy, you'll be guided every step of the way as you gain real work experience and make a real impact across the communities and industries we serve.

At Ventia, you'll be part of a supportive and collaborative team that values different perspectives and encourages you to bring your whole self to work. By working alongside experienced professionals and fellow graduates, you'll build meaningful connections, learn from industry experts and contribute to shared outcomes that make a real difference.

Together with our teams, customers and communities, you'll help deliver essential services that keep Australia and New Zealand moving.

Ventia has endless opportunities for graduates in the following disciplines:

- Bachelor Occupational Health & Safety
- Bachelor Workplace Health & Safety
- Bachelor Occupational Hygiene
- Applications from all degree backgrounds are encouraged, provided candidates can demonstrate a strong interest in Safety, Health, Environment and Quality (SHEQ).

To be eligible for the Ventia Graduate Program, you must meet the following criteria:

- Successfully completed relevant undergraduate degree in the last two years or be in your final semester of study.
- Hold Australian or New Zealand citizenship, Permanent residency, or Graduate Visa 485 (valid until the end of 2028).
- Willingness to travel and relocate for the duration of the rotation.

Responsibilities

- Support the implementation of SHEQ programs and initiatives to improve safety, quality, environmental performance, and operational excellence.
- Assist operational teams in identifying, assessing, and managing workplace risks across diverse environments.
- Participate in audits, inspections, investigations, and compliance activities to support risk management and continuous improvement.
- Analyse SHEQ data and performance trends to contribute to reporting, decision-making, and improvement initiatives.
- Collaborate with employees, leaders, contractors, and stakeholders to promote a positive safety culture and achieve SHEQ objectives.

Skills & Requirements

- Strong communication, relationship-building, and teamwork skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively with a diverse range of stakeholders.
- Highly organised, adaptable, and accountable, with the ability to manage competing priorities and deliver outcomes in fast-paced environments.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with a curious mindset and passion for continuous improvement and innovation.
- Demonstrated interest in safety, health, environment, quality, or risk management through study, work experience, projects, or extracurricular activities.
- Exposure to data analysis and reporting tools (e.g. Excel, Power BI), and experience with safety, compliance, risk, or assurance activities is highly regarded.

How to apply

To apply, click the 'apply now' button on this page and include the following:

- resume
- academic transcript,
- work rights – either Passport or Birth Certificate or Australian Citizenship Certificate and photo ID.

Application Process

The application process involves the following: application, initial screen, psychometric assessment, video interview, assessment centre, pre-employment checks & offer.
