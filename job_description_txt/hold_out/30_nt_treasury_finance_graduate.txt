Take your career somewhere unexpected

The Northern Territory Department of Treasury and Finance is looking for 10+ graduates to join our 2027 Graduate Program in Darwin.

Over 12 months, you'll complete three rotations across Treasury, work on real projects and learn alongside experienced professionals and senior leaders.

You’ll build meaningful experience early, take on real responsibility and discover where your strengths could take you.

What you'll get

- Starting salary of $76,155 + super
- Six weeks' annual recreation leave
- Three weeks' personal leave
- An additional three days' Christmas shutdown leave
- Three rotations across Treasury
- Structured learning and professional development
- Exposure to experienced professionals and senior leaders
- Opportunities to continue building your career within Treasury or the wider NT Government

Relocating to Darwin? We'll help make the move easier.
Graduate relocation support includes:

- A one-way economy flight to Darwin
- Up to 14 days' temporary accommodation
- Up to $4,000 in removal assistance
- Eligible vehicle and pet relocation costs can also be covered within the removal allowance.

Who we're looking for
We're particularly interested in graduates from:

- Economics and Finance
- Accounting, Business and Commerce
- Data, Analytics, Mathematics and Statistics
- Law and related disciplines

You can apply if you've completed an eligible degree at a recognised Australian university or institute within the past three years, or you're due to graduate before the program commences.

You'll also need to be either:

- An Australian citizen
- An Australian permanent resident, or
- A visa holder with appropriate working rights

Go further, faster
This isn't a graduate program where you'll spend 12 months watching from the sidelines.

Depending on your rotations, you could contribute to the Territory Budget, economic modelling, financial reporting, revenue forecasting, policy development, legislation, major Territory investments and advice that informs government decision-making. Rotations may include:

- Financial Management
- Economics
- Territory Revenue

You can take on significant responsibility early, with the support and supervision to learn as you go.
