McGrathNicol empowers you to make the greatest impact on your career, our performance and our clients’ future.

We are an independent professional services firm specialising in Cyber, Deals, Forensic, Performance, Restructuring, and Risk & Compliance. For more than 20 years, we have supported organisations to navigate critical moments, from growth and transformation through to financial stress and uncertainty, across the private, public and not-for-profit sectors. Guided by our values – Understanding, Commitment and Impact – we are client-focused, collaborative and committed to creating an environment where our people can do their best work and continue to grow their careers.

Our Canberra office specialises in providing tailored Advisory services to help a diverse range of Australian Government clients, ranging from peak funding bodies across various Australian Government portfolios to some of Australia’s largest Government departments. As a Data Analytics Graduate in Canberra, you will work closely with the National Data Analytics team on both Federal Government and private sector engagements. Our Data Analytics team bring together the disciplines of computer science, mathematics, statistics, econometrics, business intelligence and financial accounting with decades of combined cross-industry experience. We are seeking students with a passion and genuine interest in Data Analytics. A level of understanding or experience with Python, R, SQL, PowerBI and Alteryx is preferred but not essential.

- Our people are passionate, driven, inclusive and collaborative. Continuous development is engrained in our culture and we have robust development and reward frameworks to support performance and progression, including tailored formal training throughout your career to complement real-time coaching and feedback.
- Our Graduate Development Program is expertly designed to provide you with the technical knowledge and professional skills you need to launch your career at McGrathNicol. Over the course of your first 12 months, you will build a deep understanding of the firm and develop strong relationships with your national graduate cohort through a mix of online and in-person sessions, including key intensive 'grad camp' events throughout the year.
- We work, volunteer and celebrate as a team. Engage in the life of our firm through networking events, charity days, wellbeing initiatives and retreats.

**Who you are**

- an Australian Citizen at the time of submitting your application;
- in your final year of (or recently graduated with) a STEM related Bachelor’s or Master’s degree, preferably with a data science/analytics, business analytics, mathematics, statistics or related major; and
- available to commence full-time work in February 2027.
