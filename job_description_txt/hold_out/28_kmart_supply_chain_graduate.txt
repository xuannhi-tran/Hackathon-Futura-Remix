**Brighter futures start here!**

Ever wondered how products make their way from overseas suppliers to store shelves (and your doorstep)?

As a **Supply Chain Graduate at Kmart Group**, you’ll be right at the centre of it - learning how a large-scale retail supply chain operates end-to-end, and playing a role in making it faster, safer and more efficient for millions of customers every week.

**Why this role is for you**

This is your chance to:

- Work in a fast-paced, operational environment where no two days are the same
- See how decisions around safety, service and cost are made in real time
- Contribute to real outcomes like speed to market, product availability and safer operations
- Be part of a team transforming how our supply chain supports future growth

You’ll be joining at a pivotal moment as we continue to evolve our supply chain to support the next chapter of Kmart Group.

**What you’ll experience**

Across your rotations, you’ll gain hands-on experience across the full supply chain - learning how products are planned, moved and delivered at scale.

You’ll:

- Rotate through warehouse operations/customer fulfilment centres, transport (including online fulfilment), contract management and planning
- Learn how products move from distribution centres to stores (B2B) and customer fulfilment centres to customers (B2C)
- Use data and insights to identify opportunities to improve performance
- Build skills in problem-solving, stakeholder management and commercial thinking
- Get close to operations and see how theory translates into real business outcomes

You’ll be joining the Supply Chain team at a pivotal moment, as we continue to transform our supply chain to build the capacity and capability needed for the next chapter of growth!

**About the program**

Our 2-year graduate program gives you real experience from day one, with 3–4 rotations across the domestic supply chain and a permanent role at the end.

You’ll be supported by experienced leaders, challenged to grow, and part of a cohort learning alongside you.

Brighter futures really do start here.

**About you**

We’re looking for graduates who are curious, driven and enjoy solving problems in real-world environments.

You might be studying:

- Commerce or Business (with a major in Logistics or Supply Chain), or a related field

You’ll thrive if you:

- Enjoy working in fast-paced, operational environments
- Like using data to inform decisions
- Have a continuous improvement mindset
- Are confident communicating and working with others

**To be eligible for our 2027 program, you'll need to:**

- Be an Australian or New Zealand citizen or have Australian permanent residency at the time of application.
- Have completed an undergraduate or postgraduate degree in the past 3 years (i.e., 2024, 2025, or 2026) or be due to complete your degree by the end of January 2027.
- Be able to commence full-time employment in Melbourne in February 2027.
- Hold a driver’s licence and be open to working across our Truganina DC/CFC’s and Chadstone locations
