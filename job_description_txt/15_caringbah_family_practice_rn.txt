Description:
Caringbah Family Practice is a busy, established general practice in the Sutherland Shire with a strong focus on providing quality, patient-centred care. We believe a great workplace starts with a great team — work to create an environment where staff feel supported, appreciated, part of something bigger.

Looking for an amazing Registered Nurse — someone clinically confident, compassionate, reliable, and who enjoys having a laugh along the way. "We work hard, laugh often and occasionally wonder how it's already Friday!"

Your Daily Missions may include:
- The Vitals Detective – taking observations, blood pressures, assisting with patient assessments
- Vaccine Warrior – administering vaccinations, keeping community protected
- Wound Whisperer – wound care and dressings with compassion
- Chronic Disease Champion – supporting Care Plans, Health Assessments, chronic disease management
- Patient Cheerleader – reassuring, educating, advocating for patients
- Working collaboratively with GPs and wider practice team to provide excellent patient care
- Keeping things moving in busy, varied general practice environment
