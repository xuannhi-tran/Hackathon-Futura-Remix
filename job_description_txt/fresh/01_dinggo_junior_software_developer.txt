About DingGo
DingGo builds modern technology and services transforming automotive claims and repair workflows across Australia. We are a collaborative, growing product team looking for a Junior Software Developer who brings sound programming foundations, high curiosity, great communication skills, and a strong sense of initiative.

If you want to understand how software, business context, product decisions, and real-world users connect, rather than just writing code in a silo, you’ll thrive here.

The Opportunity
Join a tight-knit team working closely with senior engineers and technology leadership (including our CTO) to gain hands-on experience and take genuine, manageable ownership of your work. You'll start by learning our core platform (PHP / CakePHP) and business workflows, expanding into modern frontend interfaces (React) and modern tech practices over time. You will also have access to current AI-assisted development tools, supported by a team rhythm focused on using them safely, effectively, and responsibly.
What You'll Do

* Develop features, write clean code, and maintain our established PHP application while contributing to newer frontend and API interfaces as you grow
* Work with product managers, senior developers, and operational users to clarify requirements and explain technical concepts clearly
* Write tests, debug issues, perform focused workflow checks, and participate constructively in code reviews
* Dig into operational defects, figure out root causes, and keep clear documentation to help your teammates
* Actively expand your understanding across backend services, relational data, and frontend components at a sustainable, supported pace

What We're Looking For

* Practical software development experience (via employment, freelance work, or a substantial, real-world personal project)
* Ability to make complicated concepts simple and converse comfortably with both technical and non-technical teammates
* Evidence of starting useful things without waiting for every step to be mapped out for you (e.g., a maintained side project, process improvement, volunteer work, or past business venture)
* Ability to pick up new tools, frameworks, or domain logic independently and check your own work carefully
* Solid understanding of core programming concepts, basic SQL/relational databases, APIs, and Git version control

Why Join Us?

* Learn directly from experienced engineers while taking real ownership of bounded features early on
* Gain hands-on exposure to modern AI coding tools in an environment that values responsible review and output verification
* On-site access to a free gym membership and swimming pool, conveniently located right near public transport
* Regular team lunches, office socials, themed days, and team celebrations
* Full-time office attendance during your initial onboarding period, transitioning to a flexible hybrid model (minimum 2 days/week in the Rhodes office) once established
