Role Description: Are you passionate about ensuring stable, secure, and high‑performing enterprise mobility platforms? We’re looking for a Lead Engineering Operations – Enterprise Mobility professional to join our team.We are seeking a seasoned Engineering Operations professional to support and enhance large‑scale Unified Endpoint Management (UEM) & Enterprise Mobility Management (EMM) solutions/platforms (e.g. Microsoft Intune, VMware Workspace ONE, Ivanti Endpoint Manager, Ivanti Neurons) and device enrolment programs (e.g. Apple Automated Device Enrolment ADE, Samsung Knox Mobile Enrolment KME, Android Zero Touch Enrolment ZTE). You will own operational governance and service performance, lead major incidents and escalations, drive release readiness, and partner with engineering, cyber security, vendors, and stakeholders to strengthen stability, security, and compliance while delivering continuous improvement.

Roles and Responsibilities:
Mobility Operations, Change & Release Governance: Own ITIL‑aligned change control, impact assessments, and release readiness for go‑lives.
Incident, Escalation & Problem Management: Lead high‑priority incidents (incl. mission‑critical/emergency services), coordinate vendor escalations, and drive root‑cause analysis/problem management.
Security, Risk & Vulnerability Management: Partner with Cyber Security on vulnerability remediation and Mobile Threat Defence (MTD) response; manage audits and fleet posture/security event reporting.
Platform Engineering & Lifecycle Management: Lead platform uplift and optimisation initiatives across UEM/EMM services to improve reliability, scale, and user experience. Govern OS/app testing and compatibility; oversee integrations (identity, certificates, directories, network access).
Device Provisioning, Automation & Scale: Implement zero‑touch enrolment (Apple Business Manager, Android zero‑touch, Samsung Knox Mobile Enrolment) and Drive automations to streamline operations.
Leadership & Stakeholder Engagement: Provide technical leadership, mentoring, and guidance to uplift operational capability and on-call effectiveness. Manage vendor performance with clear expectations, accountability, and measurable outcomes.

Strong experience with UEM/EMM platforms such as Microsoft Intune, VMware Workspace ONE and Ivanti Neurons, including enrolment ecosystems (ADE, KME, ZTE). 
Excellent stakeholder communication skills, including executive-level incident and risk updates.
