Tyree Transformers design and manufacture Distribution and Power Transformers in Braemar NSW and as one of the leading manufacturers in the industry, we continue to experience high growth. We are proud to be wholly Australian owned and operate solely to support the charitable works of the Tyree Foundation.
Tyree is committed to supporting local talent and aim to develop opportunities for all our employees. We have generations of families across our teams and employees who have been with us for over 30 years.
Reporting to the ITC Manager, this role would be well suited to a person looking for an entry level position looking to expand their skills in growing and evolving business. 

JOB PURPOSE
·         To support and assist the Software Engineer and IT team in maintaining, developing and improving business systems, integrations and reporting tools.
·         To provide technical support and contribute to the development of applications and Power BI reports that enhance efficiency and data visibility across the organisation.
·         To ensure reliable, well-documented, and accurate business systems and reporting that support informed decision making.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
·         System Support & Administration
·         Log, manage, and resolve support requests related to business systems and applications.
·         Provide level 1 support to end users, escalating issues where required.
·         Assist in testing, configuration and troubleshooting of software, hardware, and network-related issues in conjunction with the IT Administrator and Software Engineer.
·         Maintain accurate support documentation and configuration records.
·         Provide user assistance and training on reporting tools, applications and related systems.
·         Software development & integration.
·         Work under the guidance of the Software Engineer to design, test and deploy software enhancements and system integrations.
·         Business Intelligence & Reporting
·         Ensure data integrity, consistency and accuracy across reporting platforms.
·         Work with business departments to understand reporting requirements and deliver solutions.
·         Continuous improvements & projects
·         Document technical processes, business rules and report specifications.

QUALIFICATIONS & EXPERIENCE
·         Tertiary qualification in Information Technology, Computer Science or related field.
·         Basic programming knowledge in C#, SQL or similar languages.
·         Experience or strong interest in Business Intelligence tools such as Power BI, SSRS
·         Understanding of database concepts and relational data structures (SQL preferred)
·         Familiarity with ERP systems
·         Strong analytical and problem-solving skills

KEY ATTRIBUTES
·         Eager to learn from and collaborate with IT staff
·         Strong communication skills
·         Methodical and detail-oriented approach to work
·         Team oriented with proactive mindset
·         Committed to continuous improvement
