Want to kick-start your career in law?
The Mallesons Insight Program is open to pre-penultimate law students, giving you the opportunity to gain skills, confidence and knowledge into life at Mallesons.
What you'll learn
The program consists of 3 sessions:
   * Session 1 – Panel discussion (virtual) - March 2026. We will cover how Mallesons is structured and the areas of law we practice in.
   * Session 2 - Mallesons in Practice (in-person) - date dependent on location. This is a chance to meet some of our partners and junior lawyers to hear about their experiences as legal professionals.
   * Session 3 - Clerkship application insights and tips (in-person) - date dependent on location. We will provide an exclusive insight into the clerkship recruitment process.
Total time commitment would be roughly 6 hours across 3 sessions. 
Upon completion of the Insight Program, you will have gained a solid understanding of a top-tier commercial law firm and exclusive insight into the recruitment process to help you feel prepared and confident when applying for clerkship positions the following year. 
Together we Learn. Together we Master. Together we Lead.
About you
We welcome, respect and value the diversity of cultures and experiences that individuals can bring to our ‘one firm’ culture. 
You don’t need to feel like you need to be the ‘best’ at everything. We know we are at ‘our best’ when we learn from each other’s strengths. If you are interested in gaining insight into a career in corporate or commercial law, we encourage you to apply! 
Diversity and Inclusion
Mallesons is an equal opportunity employer and is committed to diversity and inclusion. We encourage applications from Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples and people from diverse backgrounds. We welcome, value, support and harness the positive benefits that people’s different life experiences bring, especially as they relate to gender, sexual identity, culture, faith, disability and life stages. 
Am I eligible to apply? 
The Insight Program runs in Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane, Perth and Canberra.
Applications are welcome from Australian or New Zealand University law students who are in their pre-penultimate year of their university degree at the time of application (i.e. expected to graduate in 2028). Candidates must hold Australian or New Zealand citizenship or Australian permanent residency.
