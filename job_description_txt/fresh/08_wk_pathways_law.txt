Your legal career starts long before graduation.
At WK, we believe great lawyers are built through experience.

Our unique Pathways Program gives final-year law students the opportunity to spend a year working alongside talented lawyers, contributing to real client work and developing the practical skills that law school can’t teach.

Unlike a traditional clerkship, Pathways isn’t a short-term introduction to life in a law firm. It’s a year-long, apprenticeship-style experience with the only international insurance legal business.

You’ll work on real matters alongside experienced lawyers and partners, gain exposure to complex disputes and commercial challenges, and develop the legal, commercial and technology skills that will help set you apart.

Many of our senior lawyers started their careers through Pathways. It’s more than work experience. It’s a genuine pathway into a graduate role and a long-term career at WK.

What makes Pathways different?

Unlike a short-term clerkship, Pathways gives you a full year to immerse yourself in the business. Less time sitting on the sidelines and more time building practical skills, gaining confidence and contributing to meaningful work.

You’ll work closely with experienced lawyers and partners, contribute to meaningful client work and see first-hand how legal advice helps clients navigate complex challenges.

Because we’re investing in you for the long term, we’ll get to know your strengths, understand your interests and help create opportunities that support your development. As your capability grows, so will the complexity of your work.

Most importantly, Pathways isn’t designed to be a standalone student program. It’s our primary pathway into the graduate program, with many of our Pathways Paralegals going on to build successful careers at WK.

What we’re looking for

We hire carefully and deliberately.

Pathways is designed for students who want to accelerate their development, learn through experience and build a career with a business that’s constantly evolving.

We’re looking for people who take initiative, ask thoughtful questions and are motivated to keep getting better. People who embrace responsibility, enjoy solving complex problems and are excited by the opportunity to contribute from day one.

You might already have your sights set on litigation. Or you might simply enjoy thinking critically, building relationships and helping clients navigate challenging situations.

At WK, you’ll be surrounded by people who love what they do and hold themselves to a high standard. We’re looking for people who want to do the same.

Who’s eligible for the Pathways Program?
Pathways is a year-long, apprenticeship-style program designed to provide meaningful experience and prepare participants for a successful graduate career at WK. To ensure you can fully participate in the program and make the most of the opportunity, applicants must meet the following eligibility requirements:

Be an Australian citizen or permanent resident with unrestricted working rights
Be undertaking the final year of your law degree in 2027 and be on track to graduate by the end of the year
Be available to commence with WK on 8 February 2027, including participation in a full week of onboarding and induction in the office
Be available to work two to three full days per week throughout the program
Provide a complete academic transcript as part of your application
Have completed the required Priestley 11 subjects by the beginning of 2028, ensuring you’re eligible to commence Practical Legal Training (PLT) from 8 February 2028
Not be participating in another paralegal, clerkship or vacation program at the same time
