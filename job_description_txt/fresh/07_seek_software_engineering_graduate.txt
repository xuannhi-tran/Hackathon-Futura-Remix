What are you SEEKing? 

Make your mark at SEEK. Our graduate program has ranked Top 25 in Australia for three straight years—now it's your turn. 

About the Software Engineering Graduate Stream   

Our Software Engineering Graduate Program is designed to launch your technology career path. The 13-month program offers an exceptional foundation whilst gaining exposure to various technologies: AWS, GraphQL, React, TypeScript to name just a few. You will rapidly grow professionally, and immediately experience the impact your work makes on fellow SEEKers and our customers.  

When you start, you will:  

Be setup for success with our accelerator training program   
Explore two 6-month rotations where you will be embedded into teams of your choice, such as Security, Architecture, Mobile Apps or our internal start-ups  
Learn from the best with one Supervisor for the duration of each rotation who will ensure that you're on track with your career aspirations as well as being paired with a Buddy throughout the rotation to support you daily  
Select a Mentor who will guide you on your SEEK path and have a support network of current and previous SEEK grads who’ll share their experiences  
Be paid $82,000 + super + annual performance bonus 
Be based at our global HQ here in Melbourne, we hope you like ice cream!  
When you finish, the pathways are endless. SEEKers who started as grads are still here a decade later. You'll become a well-rounded, trusted advisor, and as a permanent employee from day one, you'll have access to opportunities across the entire business
