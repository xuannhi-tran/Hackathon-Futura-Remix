About the role

This is a senior leadership position for an experienced software professional who will take ownership of an existing business automation project, lead an internal development team, and personally design, develop and implement solutions through to completion and deployment.

You will take control of a major software automation project currently in progress and be responsible for reviewing existing work, establishing a clear roadmap, allocating responsibilities to the development team, and ensuring the entire solution is successfully completed and implemented into the live business environment.

You will manage a team of three software developers/engineers who will report directly to you, and report directly to the Director and CEO.

This is not purely a management position – we are looking for a CTO who remains highly hands-on and is capable of personally designing, coding, troubleshooting, integrating and implementing complex software solutions.

Key responsibilities

Review and understand existing software systems, automation project and work currently in progress

Develop a comprehensive roadmap for completing and improving the existing solution

Design a detailed and granular development pathway for each component of the system

Take ownership of the solution from concept and design through to development, testing, commissioning and handover

Personally design, develop and implement critical components of the solution

Manage and direct the existing team of three software developers/engineers

Allocate tasks, establish priorities and ensure development milestones are achieved

Redesign and improve existing solutions to make field operations and internal workflows more efficient

Manage the complete software development lifecycle, including development, testing, user feedback, refinement, final handover, implementation and live environment

Design and manage integrations between Microsoft Dynamics, Power Platform, web applications and other business systems

Troubleshoot and resolve complex technical issues independently

Maintain accountability for the quality, reliability and successful implementation of all solutions

Take ongoing ownership of the company's software automation environment following completion of the current project
