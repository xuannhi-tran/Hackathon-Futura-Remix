Build technology that makes a difference in Australian education.
Impact Curriculum is a purpose-driven, not-for-profit organisation committed to improving educational outcomes by supporting teachers with high-quality, evidence-informed curriculum resources and professional learning.
We're looking for a capable, collaborative Software Engineer who enjoys building great digital products, solving problems and making technology work better for the people who use it.
About the role
Working as part of our Technology Team, you'll help design, build and improve the digital platforms that support our work with teachers and schools across Australia.
This is a hands-on engineering role with the opportunity to contribute ideas, influence solutions and work across the full software development lifecycle.
What you'll be doing
   * Developing, testing and maintaining reliable, scalable software applications and services.
   * Building and improving APIs, integrations and application components.
   * Working with product and other colleagues to turn requirements into practical technical solutions.
   * Troubleshooting technical issues and improving system performance and reliability.
   * Contributing to code reviews, automated testing, CI/CD and engineering standards.
   * Identifying opportunities to improve our platforms, development practices and user experience.
What you'll bring
We're looking for someone with:
   * Demonstrated experience in software engineering and delivering production applications.
   * Strong programming capability and experience with contemporary development frameworks.
   * Experience with APIs, databases, integrations and cloud-based environments.
   * Sound knowledge of testing, version control and CI/CD.
   * Strong problem-solving and debugging skills.
   * An understanding of secure, maintainable software development.
   * The ability to work independently while collaborating effectively with others.
Most importantly, you'll be curious, practical and committed to building quality technology that makes a difference.
Why join Impact Curriculum?
   * Purpose: Your work will contribute to better educational outcomes for Australian teachers and students.
   * Flexibility: Work remotely from anywhere in Australia. Full-time preferred, with part-time arrangements considered.
   * Impact: See the direct contribution your work makes to products used in education.
   * Collaboration: Work with a passionate, multidisciplinary team that values ideas, initiative and continuous improvement.
   * Opportunity: Contribute to the development and evolution of meaningful digital products.
