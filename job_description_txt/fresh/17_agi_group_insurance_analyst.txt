AGI is a highly regarded, growing third-party insurance administrator (TPA) providing administrative services and web-based solutions to the financial services industry. We offer a warm and supportive work environment and rigorous training and development to ensure our people succeed in their roles. Due to continued growth, we are looking for insurance analysts to join our high-performing dynamic team in a full-time office based position.

About the role

As a group insurance analyst, you will provide a wide range of support services to brokers and insurers while contributing to the continuous improvement of our internal operations.

Applicants must be Australian citizens or permanent residents with full working rights.

Reporting to a team leader who will provide support and oversight, you will be the primary contact for brokers and the insurers for the general administration of a portfolio of the insurer's policies. This role will expose you to stimulating work that will challenge you and hone your analytical and problem-solving skills.

At the beginning of your journey at AGI you will be paired with a mentor who will train you in all aspects of the role. They will support the development of your skills, enabling you to work independently and achieve the highest levels of competency.

Your responsibilities will include, but are not limited to:

applying actuarial knowledge to prepare group insurance reviews and reports as required by local authorities on behalf of our clients and external audits

handling premium estimation and preparing quotes for corporate clients; assisting clients with their insurance expense projections

assessing and analysing risk in relation to insurance products using actuarial assessment under guidelines and delegated authority by the insurers

analysing and investigating data, including review and evaluation of data integrity and data sources, and implementing processes and procedures to improve data quality for valuation and reporting purposes

liaising with advisers and brokers to address queries related to premium calculations, data collection, pricing, and policy structures

improving the business process efficiency and compliance continuously, involving making suggestions for process enhancement, analysing business logic, implementing processes, adapting current processes to new requirements, and performing UAT

assisting leadership in facilitating business decision-making in situations where actuarial technical problems are related.

To succeed in this role, you are:

a team player with a positive attitude and who enjoys helping others

self-motivated, with an excellent work ethic

responsive and open to feedback

enthusiastic about learning and developing your skills

a critical thinker with an analytical mind

Additionally, you will have:

very strong numeracy skills with exceptionally high attention to detail

high familiarity with Microsoft Excel

intermediate proficiency in Microsoft Excel including advanced formulas and data analysis functions

fluency in English, especially the ability to write to a professional standard

the ability to apply knowledge to solve problems and mitigate issues

good time management

Desirable but not essential:

relevant experience in similar fields of work or strong academic results in actuarial science, accounting, business, IT, engineering, or mathematics (bachelor's degree or higher).
