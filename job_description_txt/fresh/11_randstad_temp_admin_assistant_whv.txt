Temporary Administrative Assistant - 2 week contract starting immediately

Location: Parramatta, NSW (Full-time, On-site)
Contract Type: Casual / Temporary (2-Week Assignment)
Start Date: Immediate
Working Holiday Visas (WHV) Welcome!
About the Company
Randstad is partnering with a well-established, boutique wealth management firm based in Parramatta. The organisation pride themselves on delivering premium, personalised service to their clients, backed by a strong culture of professionalism, integrity, and operational excellence.
About the Role
We are seeking a reliable, detail-oriented Temporary Administrative Assistant to join the team for a two-week project focused on document digitisation and records management.
Working on-site in our Parramatta office, you will play a key role in transitioning physical records into our secure electronic systems while maintaining strict compliance and confidentiality standards.
Key Responsibilities

* Document Scanning & Uploading: Systematically scan and upload client records into our online electronic filing system.
* File & Data Verification: Review files and records to ensure high accuracy and complete data capture.
* Original Document Separation: Identify, catalog, and separate essential original physical documents that require physical retention.
* Records Maintenance: Assist with the general organization and structured maintenance of both physical archive boxes and electronic filing systems.
* General Admin Support: Provide ad-hoc administrative help to the team as needed.

What We Are Looking For

* High Attention to Detail: Comfortable processing large volumes of paperwork efficiently without compromising accuracy.
* Utmost Discretion: Ability to handle highly sensitive and confidential client information with absolute professionalism and integrity.
* Reliability: Punctual, focused, and able to work productively in an office environment.
* Availability: Ready for an immediate start and able to work full-time hours on-site in Parramatta for the full 2-week duration.
* Visa Requirements: Full working rights in Australia required (Working Holiday Visa holders are encouraged to apply!).
