About the role
MSS Security is seeking Security Officers who hold or are eligible for a Positing Vetting Clearance to join our team in Sydney. We have positions available at a high-profile data centre, with sponsorship available for candidates who do not yet hold a clearance.

Employees holding a Positive Vetting Clearance will receive a competitive allowance of up to $20,000 per year.

Duties include but are not limited to:

* Conducting foot patrols, ensuring people are safe and the building is secure.
* Monitor and control access to the premises, ensuring only authorised individuals enter.
* Oversee CCTV systems to detect and respond to security threats.
* Provide excellent customer service and assist visitors with inquiries.
* Respond promptly and professionally to security incidents or emergencies.

Requirements:

* Must be an Australian Citizen.
* Hold a current AGSVA Positive Vetting Clearance or willing to obtain.
* Valid NSW Security Licence.
* Valid NSW Drivers Licence.
* Valid First Aid & CPR Certificates or willing to obtain.
* 24/7 Availability to work a rotating roster.
* Ability to walk up to 15,000 steps per shift as part of routine patrols.
* Must be able to supply background information including (but not limited to) referees, past addresses, overseas travel, education etc.
